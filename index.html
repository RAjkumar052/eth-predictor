Model methodology
Training: chronological walk-forward validation followed by training on the available historical dataset.

Inputs: ETH momentum, EMA trend, RSI, volume, candle range, ATR volatility, BTC momentum/trend/RSI, and ETH/BTC relative movement.

Classes: Up / Neutral / Down using a point-in-time volatility threshold.

Validation: accuracy, balanced accuracy, log loss and majority-class baseline are calculated from future observations that were not used to train each validation fold.
