V15.2 architecture
All available market sources are normalized into one realtime feature set. Online models update their state from new market observations. A prediction is locked at creation time; after its target time, the actual ETH price is compared with the forecast and the result becomes training feedback. The browser does not claim that additional data guarantees accuracy.
Learning safeguards
No future candles are used before their timestamps. Historical data and live data are kept time ordered. Source outages are marked unavailable instead of silently treating missing data as zero. Model performance is tracked separately for each horizon.
